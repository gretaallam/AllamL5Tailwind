The Best Energy Drinks:Opinions from a Pro

Disclaimer

I'm not actually a professional. I'm not that addicted, I swear... Also this is a Rockstar and Alani free zone.

Drink #1:

Monster Zero Ultra

It's gotta be the best energy drink made. A classic. I still don't know what flavour it is supposed to be, which is unfortunate because people have asked me.

Rating: 10 / 10

Drink #2:

Redbull White Peach

This might be the best thing I've ever had in my life. Not even joking. It loses a point only because Redbulls are too small. I did take this photo.

Rating: 9.9 / 10

Drink #3:

Redbull Watermelon

First Redbull I've ever had and it's pretty good. No complaints, but I only ever get it if it's the only good redbull there.

Rating: 8 / 10

Drink #4:

Monster Ultra Vice Guava

Super tasty. It reminds me of living in Dryden when I drank one everyday to survive being a summer camp counsellor.

Rating: 9 / 10

Drink #5:

Monster Ultra Strawberry Dreams

This image describes it perfectly. I actually can't remember what it tastes like because it has been a while, but I know it's so good.

Rating: 8.5 / 10

Drink #6:

Monster Ultra Paradise

If you couldn't tell I like Monster Ultra. This one reminds me of being 14 and it's one of the first ones I've had. It is very sweet and a bit funky, but good nontheless.

Rating: 8 / 10

Drink #7:

Celcius Kiwi Guava

I had to include this one because it's the first Celcius I ever drank and I had no idea it was an energy drink. It is also the only Celcius flavour worth drinking.

Rating: 7.8 / 10

Drink #8:

Monster ASSAULT

Worst thing I've ever tasted. Had to include it as a warning. It's the only drink I've ever dumped out immediately. If someone enjoys Monster Assault then I am staying far far away from them. Half a point for the name being accurate.

Rating: 0.5 / 10

Warning

I don't recommend drinking these. Save yourself. #broke
